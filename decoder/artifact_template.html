CVQR/1

A human voice, compressed to a few hundred bytes and carried inside an ordinary QR code. Paste the payload or hand it a photograph of the code, and it reconstructs the recording here in this tab — no upload, no account, and no network once this page has loaded.

Recovered

The payload is plain text beginning CVQR1: — Base45 (RFC 9285) wrapping a small binary capsule of Codec2 speech. Any generic QR scanner will show you that text; this page turns it back into sound.

It is not encrypted. Anyone holding the code can hear what it says, which is the intent. Integrity is checked with CRC-32 before anything plays, so a damaged payload is refused rather than played back as noise.

Codec2 by David Rowe (LGPL-2.1), compiled to WebAssembly. QR reading by jsQR (Apache-2.0). Both are bundled into this page rather than fetched from anywhere.
