CVQR/1

A voice recording carried inside an ordinary QR code. Everything below happens in this tab — nothing is uploaded, and once the page has loaded it needs no network at all.

The payload is plain text beginning CVQR1: — Base45 (RFC 9285) wrapping a small binary capsule of Codec2 speech. It is not encrypted: anyone holding the code can hear it. That is the point.

Integrity is checked with CRC-32 before anything plays. A damaged payload is refused rather than played as noise.

The format is specified in full at /FORMAT.md. Codec2 by David Rowe (LGPL-2.1). QR reading by jsQR (Apache-2.0). Both are bundled here, not fetched.
